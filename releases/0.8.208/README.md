# Morph TV 0.8.208

- IPTV Home uses uniform, larger poster cards ordered Discover, Movies, Series, Continue Watching, Live Sports.
- Hero rotates available provider movie/series artwork and matching metadata. Play resolves the selected title or episode.
- Sidebar appears on Home only. Larger top navigation remains usable in catalogs; redundant account headings removed.
- Visible season chips complement the episode selector and remote navigation.
- Android player removes duplicate 10-second buttons, strengthens focus highlighting and adds movie/episode browsing alongside live channels.
- Hidden legacy HDMI image is explicitly suppressed to avoid duplicate icon frames.

Validation: Android TV and phone APK compilation/signature verification; LG packaging; automated browser checks on phone and TV sizes in Arabic and English, progressive/cached loading, focus, scoped favorites and refresh deadlines.

Limits: Provider artwork and episode metadata depend on the subscription. Native episode overlay and playback have not been physically verified on a TV in this release. Tizen 2 remains the separate experimental 0.8.206 candidate; Samsung WGT downloads require signing for the target television DUID.
